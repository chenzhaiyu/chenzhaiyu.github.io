I am a PhD student at [TU Munich](https://www.tum.de/en/), advised by Prof. [Xiao Xiang Zhu](https://scholar.google.de/citations?user=CNakdIgAAAAJ) at the [Chair of Data Science in Earth Observation](https://www.asg.ed.tum.de/en/sipeo/home/). I am affiliated with [Munich Center for Machine Learning](https://www.mcml.ai/) and supported by [TUM Georg Nemetschek Institute](https://www.mdsi.tum.de/gni/about-gni/). I received my MSc in [Geomatics](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/education/programmes/masters/gm/msc-geomatics/) from [TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/), advised by Prof. [Liangliang Nan](https://3d.bk.tudelft.nl/liangliang/) in the [3D Geoinformation Group](https://3d.bk.tudelft.nl/), and a BEng in Remote Sensing from [Wuhan University](https://en.whu.edu.cn/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of 3D computer vision and machine learning, with applications in remote sensing of the built environment. <span class="job-market-accent">I will be on the job market in late 2026 and would welcome discussions about opportunities!</span> 👋
