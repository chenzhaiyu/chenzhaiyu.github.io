I am a PhD student at [TU Munich](https://www.tum.de/en/), advised by Prof. [Xiao Xiang Zhu](https://scholar.google.de/citations?user=CNakdIgAAAAJ) at the [Chair of Data Science in Earth Observation](https://www.asg.ed.tum.de/en/sipeo/home/) and affiliated with the [Munich Center for Machine Learning](https://www.mcml.ai/). I am currently an academic visitor at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), hosted by Prof. [Konrad Schindler](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FZuNgqIAAAAJ) in the [Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing Group](https://prs.igp.ethz.ch/).


I received my MSc in [Geomatics](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/education/programmes/masters/gm/msc-geomatics/) from [TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/), advised by Prof. [Liangliang Nan](https://3d.bk.tudelft.nl/liangliang/) in the [3D Geoinformation Group](https://3d.bk.tudelft.nl/), and a BEng in Remote Sensing from [Wuhan University](https://en.whu.edu.cn/).
